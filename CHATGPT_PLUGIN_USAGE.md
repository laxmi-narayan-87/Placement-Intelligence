# How to Use Placement Intelligence in ChatGPT

Placement Intelligence is a personal placement-intelligence plugin for finding fresh, verified, actionable India software and technology hiring information.

## Step 1 — Download the repository

1. Open the **Placement Intelligence** GitHub repository.
2. Click **Code**.
3. Click **Download ZIP**.
4. Extract the downloaded ZIP file on your computer.

## Step 2 — Open ChatGPT

1. Open ChatGPT on the web.
2. Sign in to your ChatGPT account.
3. Open the workspace/account where you have permission to upload plugins.

## Step 3 — Open Plugins

1. Open **Plugins** in ChatGPT.
2. Open the plugin management area.
3. Look for **Add** or **+**.

> The exact menu names can vary depending on your ChatGPT plan, workspace, permissions, and current interface.

## Step 4 — Upload the plugin

1. Click **Upload plugin**.
2. Select the downloaded **Placement Intelligence ZIP file**.
3. Wait for ChatGPT to process the plugin.
4. Follow any setup or installation prompts.

## Step 5 — Add the plugin

1. Review the plugin information.
2. Click **Add** or **Install** if shown.
3. Confirm that **Placement Intelligence** is available in ChatGPT.

## Step 6 — Try Chat

1. Start a new ChatGPT conversation.
2. Select **Placement Intelligence** from the available plugin/app options.
3. If @-mentions are available in your interface, type:
   `@Placement Intelligence`

## Step 7 — Run your first command

Use:

> **@Placement Intelligence**  
> Give me today's placement updates using Placement Intelligence. Prioritize fresh, verified, actionable India software/technology hiring information for my profile.

## Step 8 — Try useful commands

### Find new jobs

```
@Placement Intelligence
Find new software/technology jobs posted in the last 3 days
that match my profile.
```

### Check whether a job is live

```
@Placement Intelligence
Check whether this job is still live, who is eligible,
the location, deadline, and application link.
```

### Get a placement briefing

```
@Placement Intelligence
Give me a 5-minute placement briefing for today.
```

### Check deadlines

```
@Placement Intelligence
Show me placement applications with approaching deadlines.
```

### Check what changed

```
@Placement Intelligence
What changed in India's software hiring since yesterday?
Focus only on actionable placement information.
```

## Step 9 — Verify before applying

Before applying, check:

- Company
- Role
- Current/open status
- Eligibility
- Graduation year
- Experience requirement
- Location
- Deadline
- Assessment/interview details
- Official application link

Placement Intelligence should prioritize official company and application sources and clearly distinguish information as **Confirmed**, **Reported**, or **Unverified**.

## Step 10 — Apply

Once the opportunity is verified:

**Placement Intelligence → Verify → Open application → Apply**

Always check the company's official application page before submitting an application.

## Recommended daily workflow

1. **Morning:** Ask for today's placement briefing.
2. **Find:** Ask for new jobs matching your profile.
3. **Filter:** Check eligibility and location.
4. **Verify:** Confirm that the job post is still live.
5. **Act:** Apply to suitable verified opportunities.
6. **Evening:** Ask what changed since the morning.

## Important

Placement Intelligence is designed for research and verification. It does not guarantee that a job is available after the last verification, so always confirm the current status on the official company or application page.

The exact names and locations of **Plugins**, **Add**, **Upload plugin**, and related controls may vary across ChatGPT plans, workspaces, permissions, and interface versions.
